# BKK information needed before the live launch

The sample events, offers and services currently shown in the website and Android app are **demonstrations**, not promises to members. A BKK staff member must check each replacement record before an administrator publishes it. Leave a field blank if it is not yet known; do not guess.

## One event per form

- Event title and plain-language description:
- Date, start time, end time and time zone:
- Venue, full address and access/directions:
- Category and contact person:
- Is attendance limited? If yes, capacity and who confirms attendance:
- Is the event public and approved for website/app publication? Name and date of approval:
- Who will tell the administrator if it changes or is cancelled?

## One discount per participating business

- Business name and authorised contact person:
- Exact discount and which items/services qualify:
- Who is eligible and what proof, if any, must a member show?
- Start date, expiry date, participating branches and exclusions:
- How the member claims it; any limits or stock conditions:
- Written business permission to publish these terms: approver, date and evidence location:
- Who will notify BKK if the offer changes or ends?

Do not publish an offer or send a phone alert until the business has approved the exact wording and dates. An administrator should test the public card and Android details after publishing.

## BKK-wide approval

- Approved organisation name, logo and image-use permission:
- Public contact address and phone number:
- Privacy notice, retention period and responsible contact approved by BKK:
- Staff member authorised to approve public information:
- Date of final content sign-off:

Keep the original approvals with BKK's project records. Do not put private staff phone numbers, member data or account credentials in GitHub. After approval, the administrator can replace the demo records, check the public website, and refresh the Android app to verify both show the same information.
