# About page and homepage introduction

## Approved brief — 16 October 2026

The user approved using BKK Community Group, its non-profit purpose serving older community members in South Africa, and founder Andrew Spaumer. The original task is to explain who BKK is and what the website/app do, rather than present feature buttons without context.

- Goal: make the organisation and the platform understandable to a first-time visitor.
- Audience: older community members and people helping them access information.
- Value: events, senior-discount information, local-service information and enquiries in one place.
- Main About-page action: Explore community events. Secondary action: Contact BKK.
- Context: visitors may be seeing BKK for the first time, including through the presentation/download QR. This is an editorial assumption, not a measured traffic statistic.

## Sources and claim limits

The client's Phase 1 planning documentation identifies BKK Community Group as a South African NPO serving elderly community members and identifies Andrew Spaumer as founder. Those details also appear in the supplied Phase 3/4 development documentation. The user confirmed the brief before publication.

No expansion of the letters BKK, founding date, registration number, exact service area, membership totals, unverified telephone/email details, testimonials or formal merchant partnerships were added. The copy describes the platform's purpose and functionality, not independently verified impact or guaranteed benefits. It does not describe sample discounts as merchant-approved offers.

## Published structure

- Homepage headline: **Stay connected with BKK**.
- Homepage introduction: identifies the group, its audience and the information offered.
- About headline: **About BKK Community Group**.
- Who we are: organisation, founder and audience.
- Why this platform exists: scattered information and participation/access difficulties.
- What you can do here: events, discounts, local support and enquiries.
- Straightforward design: readable text, clear labels and simple choices.
- Information management: authorised staff maintain the central system; offline reads and online actions are distinguished.
- Final action: Explore community events, with contact and app-download links.

## Wording options considered

Headline alternatives:

1. **Stay connected with BKK** — selected: short, names the community and fits the homepage without obscuring task choices.
2. **Community information, all in one place** — clear functional alternative, but does not identify the organisation on its own.
3. **Find your next community activity** — concrete, but too narrow for discounts and local support.

Primary About-page action alternatives:

1. **Explore community events** — selected: describes the destination and a useful first step.
2. **Find an event** — shorter, suitable for the events feature card.
3. **See upcoming activities** — understandable alternative, but existing site navigation uses Events.

The chosen copy favours plain language over slogans, links each feature to a practical use and avoids invented proof. The About page is reachable from the main menu, homepage and footer. The main menu retains five content destinations; account access is a separate, always-visible header button.

## Checks

Web smoke coverage checks public access, key approved facts, purpose text, homepage introduction, active About navigation, separate account access and exclusion of unverified contact details. Local browser checks at a 360px viewport confirmed a five-link mobile menu including About and no horizontal page overflow. Desktop and live deployment checks must be reported separately from these local checks.
