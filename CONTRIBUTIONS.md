## Contribution rules

- Every member uses their own GitHub account.
- Commits must describe real work.
- Results must include dates, devices or browsers and evidence.
- Pull requests must be reviewed by another member.
- Commit dates and authors must not be falsified.
- Pair-programmed work may use `Co-authored-by` only when both members genuinely worked on it.
